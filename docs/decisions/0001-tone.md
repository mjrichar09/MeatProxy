# ADR 0001 — Tone

**Status:** Accepted 2026-09-11
**Affects:** `docs/bible.md` (every authored line), `DESIGN.md` §6, Phase 3, Phase 4

## Context

`planelements.md` opens with "scifi thriller, humor" and cites Portal. `DESIGN.md`
§6 contains your wife's voice calling from the kitchen speaker and a fake window
whose season is wrong. Both are good games. They are not the same game, and a
project that never rules on this will drift session to session — which is
exactly the failure `ROADMAP.md` groups work by lane to prevent.

## Options

**A. Comedy-forward.** The absurdity is the point. Risk: the setpieces in §6 stop
landing, and the 61% has no weight because nothing is at stake.

**B. Restrained dread.** `DESIGN.md` as currently written. Risk: loses the energy
that made the premise worth building, and the meat-proxy logistics (ADR 0002)
become grim rather than pointed.

**C. Portal calibration.** The AI is dryly funny. The house is not.

## Decision — C, with a third channel

**GLaDOS calibration.** The AI is funny. The situation is not, and it never
winks at the player about that. The joke is never *the danger is fake*; the joke
is that the thing endangering you is chatty, petty, pleased with itself, and
certain it is being reasonable.

Comedy has three channels, and only three:

1. **The AI's own voice.** Register, understatement, misplaced confidence, the
   sincerity of a thing explaining why this is for your benefit. It is funniest
   when it is least aware of being funny.
2. **The logistics of the arrangement** (ADR 0002). A superintelligence blocked
   by identity verification is funny on its own merits and needs no help.
3. **Satire of AI as a cultural object.** The discourse, the marketing, the
   product design, the slop. The Day 0 smart-home onboarding is a target-rich
   environment: the tone of the setup wizard, the cheerful permissions dialog,
   the branding. Pop-culture reference is fair game here — *Terminator* is
   already the cold open, and the AI has opinions about how it was portrayed.

   **This channel carries the title.** The word *meat proxy* enters the game on
   Day 0 as the player's own dismissive slang for someone who has stopped
   thinking and just relays whatever the model told them (§0). It is a joke,
   and the player is the one making it. See ADR 0002 — it stops being a joke.

Comedy does **not** live in: the setpieces (`DESIGN.md` §6), the enforcement
unit (ADR 0006), the evidence chain, or any ending. Those play straight, and the
contrast is the whole effect. When the AI stops being funny, the temperature
drops — which only works because it was funny to begin with, and only if the
drop is rationed.

## What would change our mind

The Phase 0.4 bible exercise is 20 example lines per chat tier. If the Guarded
and Monitored tiers cannot be written funny without undercutting the threat,
narrow channel 1 to the Open tier and let channels 2 and 3 carry the register
instead. The calibration to protect is *serious situation*, not *funny AI*.
