Before you start

Two short exercises, about five minutes each. There are no wrong answers and nothing is being scored — we're testing the interface, not you. Work at your own pace and say whatever you're thinking out loud as you go.

One — Leaving a note for the house

Composition

You can talk to the house any time — it is listening. It also reads things: parcel labels, the calendar, network names, whatever is in front of a camera. Start by just asking it.

What we're listening for

You could have just asked it. Why didn't that work — and did the house make that clear, or did you have to guess?

  • Which surface felt most like writing something, and which felt like guessing a password?
  • The whiteboard behaves differently from the label. Did you work out why?
  • Where did you get stuck, and what did you expect to happen?

Two — An ordinary week

Day loop

You're at home. You have things you mean to get to. Play a few days — pick whatever you actually feel like doing. Keep going until the console says you're finished.
